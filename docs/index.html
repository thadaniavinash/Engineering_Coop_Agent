How this works

Every morning at about 7 a.m., a free GitHub Actions job searches the job boards and company careers sites listed above. It keeps student roles in Ontario that match the keywords, pulls out the term length, start date, pay and deadline where the posting states them, and updates this page. New listings are emailed. Once a week, Claude writes the short summaries and searches the web for employers the list doesn't cover yet.

Nothing is thrown away just because the posting is vague: those listings are marked Needs check. Distances are straight-line from Newmarket; driving is usually 20–30% farther. Your shortlist and status marks are saved in this browser only, so use Export CSV to keep a copy or move them to another device.
